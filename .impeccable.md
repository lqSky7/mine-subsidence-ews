## Design Context

### Users
Hackathon demonstrators and mine-control operators use the dashboard to notice changing sensor conditions, understand why an early warning was raised, and decide whether to inspect the test rig.

### Brand Personality
Calm, industrial, and evidence-first. The interface should build confidence through legible sensor evidence rather than spectacle or alarm theatrics.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the established industrial monitoring palette: neutral surfaces, amber for early attention, and restrained red only for confirmed critical states. Keep motion minimal and functional.

### Design Principles
- Put the signal, confidence, and contributing sensors before generic AI language.
- Escalate calmly: stable, watch, then critical.
- Make the model's limits visible: this is an anomaly indicator, not a subsidence confirmation.
- Preserve dense, scan-friendly operator layouts across desktop and mobile.
- Do not depend on colour alone to convey status.
